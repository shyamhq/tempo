Problem

Album list and detail endpoints hit Supabase 3–5 times per request. listAlbums fires three queries; getAlbumDetail fires six.

Outcome

Album list and detail pages load faster on repeat visits — the hot path hits an in-memory cache instead of Supabase.

Success criteria

  • Creating or deleting an album invalidates the requesting user's list cache
  • Updating an album invalidates that user's detail cache
